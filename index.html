5. SWOT analysis

Strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats for each company, drawn from the same research base as section 4.

DoorDash

Current employer
Strengths
  • Dominant US restaurant-delivery order share (~56–65%) vs. Uber Eats (~23%) and Grubhub (~16%)
  • Ad revenue "substantially larger" than $100M/year and growing, expanding into grocery/CPG advertisers
  • International scale via Wolt + Deliveroo spanning ~40 countries
  • Adjusted EBITDA +40% YoY in Q2 2026 even while absorbing heavy integration spend
Weaknesses
  • GAAP net income fell 30% YoY (Q2 2026) despite revenue growth — margin compression from investment spend
  • Wound down Deliveroo/Wolt operations in four countries (Feb 2026) — uneven international execution
  • Some employees describe promotions as "harder to come by" recently (Blind)
  • Thin restaurant-side margins persist industry-wide, a structural constraint on the core business
Opportunities
  • Advertising and grocery/retail diversification (SevenRooms acquisition, $1.2B, 2025)
  • New global tech platform targeted for H1 2027 to unlock international synergies
  • Large remaining international TAM despite the recent four-country retrenchment
Threats
  • NYC's Jan 2026 gig-worker law (guaranteed minimum pay, deactivation-appeal rights) may set a precedent other cities follow
  • Investor skepticism about 2026 spend vs. near-term GAAP profit — the dominant narrative behind the ~40% pullback from highs
  • Uber Eats and Instacart competitive pressure in adjacent categories

Square (Block, Inc.)

Prospective employer
Strengths
  • Square segment GPV and gross profit growth accelerating — "strongest since Q2 2023" as of Q2 2026
  • Cash App is now the larger profit engine: gross profit +31% YoY, lending originations +59% YoY
  • FY26 guidance raised across gross profit, operating income, and EPS
  • Analyst consensus firmly "Buy" (37 buy / 7 hold / 0 sell of ~40 analysts)
Weaknesses
  • "Career opportunities" rating (3.1/5 Glassdoor) is a consistent weak point company-wide
  • Two rounds of layoffs in 12 months (~8% Mar 2025, ~10% Feb 2026) — only 46% of reviewers would recommend it to a friend
  • $80M multistate BSA/AML settlement (Jan 2025) over Cash App compliance failures, incl. a ~170,000-alert unprocessed SAR backlog
  • Seller-side competition from Toast, Clover, and Shopify POS squeezing the core Square merchant base
Opportunities
  • Aggressive 2026 Bitcoin/crypto push (Bitkey wallet, fee-free BTC purchases, merchant tap-to-pay BTC acceptance)
  • Consumer lending expansion (+59% YoY originations) via AI-driven underwriting
  • Cash App banking/card products capturing a larger share of members' financial lives
Threats
  • Stock is far more volatile than DASH — 52-week range spans ~1.8x, incl. an 18% dip in one 90-day stretch in 2026
  • Ongoing regulatory remediation tied to the AML settlement (9-month review + 12-month remediation plan)
  • Recurring layoffs cited internally as the biggest stress factor — "rolling layoffs and re-orgs" (Blind)
  • Trailing 12-month stock return (~11%) has meaningfully lagged DoorDash's (~54–67%) over the same window
